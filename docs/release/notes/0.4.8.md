Kolahal 0.4.8, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.7 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.8.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.8-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap) and no calls in the log, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **Your own name in name@device**: Settings → Home screen → **Your name** (`user.name`) replaces `kolahal` in the header's device name and at the top of `fetch`. It's one word of up to 32 characters, without spaces or `@`; the field turns red until it is one.

**kolahal-0.4.8-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
