Kolahal 0.4.6, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.5 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.6.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.6-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no notifications in the log, no media controls and no calls in the log. Play Protect blocks sideloaded apps that have an accessibility service or notification access (in India and some other countries); this one has neither, so take it if the full build won't install. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## Fixed

- **Wallpaper showing** takes effect at once: turned on while home was open, the wallpaper didn't show (and old lines could smear) until Kolahal restarted.
- With the wallpaper showing, a log line you swipe stays see-through instead of a solid band, and the charging bolt in the battery icon is cut out rather than painted in the page's colour.

**kolahal-0.4.6-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
