Kolahal 0.4.7, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.6 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.7.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.7-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap) and no calls in the log, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **`script diff <name>`**: a library script you changed, against the library version, in the log, as **Compare** shows it in the script manager: lines only in the library version in red (`-`), lines only in yours in the theme's colour (`+`), three unchanged ones around each change and the rest counted. Copy text on its first line copies it as a plain diff, and its last line says how to take the library version.
- **The app selector's row height**: Settings → App selector → **Row height** (`selector.row-height`, 32 to 64 dp, 38 as before) spaces its list out; while you drag its slider, home shows the list open behind Settings.
- **The calculator answers at once**: arithmetic after `=` is worked out on the phone instead of in Lua, and the answer shows above the prompt while you type; tap it to go on from it. Percents work as on a phone's calculator (`= 15% of 80`, `= 80 + 15%`), and so do `×`, `÷`, `mod`, `n!`, `sqrt`, `ln`, `log` and the like, without `math.`. `= 2^10` says `1024`, not `1024.0`. Lua code after `=` still runs as before.
- **Find commands and settings by what they do**: a word that isn't a command's name also suggests the commands and settings whose description has it, after the apps: `flash` offers `torch`, `silent` offers `ring`, `music` offers `media`, `24` the 24-hour clock setting. Your script commands' `@desc` counts too.
- **Reminders on a date, and reminders that repeat**: `remind dec 3 10:30 dentist` (also `3 dec`, `2027-12-03`, with a year or not), and `remind every day 8:00 pills`, `every weekday`, `every weekend`, `every mon,thu 19:00 bins`, `every month 1st rent` or `every year mar 14 birthday`. `reminders` says how each repeats; `remind rm` ends it. The reminder notification has **Snooze 10 min**, **Snooze 1 hour** and **Done** buttons.
- **App search forgives slips**: when nothing matches what you typed, a letter wrong, missing or extra, or two swapped still finds the app (`mpas` for Maps), and accents don't matter (`cafe` finds Café). A slip never launches an app as you type.
- **Apps you use now rank first**: each launch counts one and that halves every week. Before, an app opened fifty times months ago jumped back to the top after one launch, above the app you open every day.
- **Script options**: a script's `@option` lines are settings you change without editing its code, in the script's sheet (**Options**) or with `script set <name> <option> <value>`; the script reads them as `options.city`. A choice (`metric|imperial`) shows as chips. Your values stay through library updates and renames, and go into backups.
- **The script editor shows a run's lines, runs with args or as an event, and checks on save**: a run's output shows under the code. A long press on **[run]** runs it with words as `args`, or as one of its `@on` events or `@at` times with made-up values. Saving applies a new or changed `@every` or `@at` line to the script's timers, and points out names the API doesn't have, calls that need a `@uses` line, and `@` lines it can't read, with their line. `script run <name> <words>` passes the words as `args`.
- **Automatic backups**: Settings → Backup → **Back up on its own** (`backup.auto`), or `backup every daily|weekly`, writes the backup file to a folder you pick, `kolahal-backup-<date>.json`, keeping the newest 7 (`backup.keep`). `backup` writes one now, `backup dir` picks the folder, and Settings has **Backup folder** and **Back up now**. A failed backup leaves a line in the log.
- **Moving to a new phone carries everything**: Android's transfer from your old phone (cable or Wi-Fi, Android 12 and newer) now brings scripts, todos, notes, reminders, feeds and the log, not only settings.
- **Share your themes**: the theme builder's export icon saves one of your themes in a `.json` file, and Settings → Look & feel → Your themes → **Import a theme** adds one from such a file and puts it in use. A theme by the same name that you already have stays: the imported one gets a number.

## Changed

- **The Play build doesn't read your contacts**: Google Play wants apps to use Android's contact picker when it's enough. In the Play build, `call`, `sms` and `contacts` with no name, or a name Kolahal doesn't know yet, open the picker, and Kolahal carries on with the person you pick: it dials them, starts the message with your text, or shows them in the log. It remembers who you pick, on the phone, so `call asha` works by itself next time and they're your suggestions; `contacts --forget <name>` forgets someone. Contacts you allowed in an earlier Play build aren't read any more: pick the people you call once. The full build is unchanged.
- **The app selector eases between sliding and the list left open**: when you lift your thumb, the magnified apps around it shrink back and the other letters' apps fade back in, instead of the list jumping flat at once. Touching down and moving between the edge and the list ease the same way.

## Fixed

- **The highlighted suggestion is what Enter does**: a command typed in full (`clear`, `torch`) is the first row, marked ⏎, above its options. Before, `clear history` was marked while Enter cleared the screen, and `torch on` while Enter toggled the torch. When Enter runs the line as you typed it, no row is marked.
- **`clear` can be undone**: `clear` and `Ctrl+L` offer **Undo** under the log for a few seconds, as deleting a line does. Until then the cleared notifications, feed items and script lines are only out of view; before, they were deleted at once.
- **Every chat message reaches the log**: a message that came less than 30 seconds after the one before it in the same chat was left out, so "are you free?" showed and "never mind, call me now" didn't. Other apps' notifications that change within 30 seconds still keep their first line.
- **Timed scripts that don't go online run offline too**: a script with `@every` waited for a network on every run, even one that never makes a request, such as a battery log. Now only scripts that use `http` or `rss` wait for one, as daily `@at` runs already did.
- **`help <command>`** shows that command's usage, what it does and its other names, and a line to the guide; it used to print the whole list whatever followed it. A word that isn't a command lists the nearest ones, and after `help ` the suggestions offer the commands.
- **`remind fri 8am` on a Friday morning is today**, not a week later; a weekday whose time has passed today is next week's.
- **Open the keyboard on home** (`keyboard.on-home`) opens it whenever home comes back, from an app or the lock screen too, not only when you press Home.
- **`pin` says where a pinned app shows**: first in the suggestions on an empty prompt only when `suggestions.idle` is on, and starred in the app selector when it is on.

## Removed

- **The paper texture** (`theme.paper`, config only): home's background is the theme's own colour. A saved value is dropped, and `config` says it's gone if you ask for it.

**kolahal-0.4.7-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
