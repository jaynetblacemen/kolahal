Kolahal 0.4.2, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.1 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.2.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.2-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no notifications in the log, no media controls and no calls in the log. Play Protect blocks sideloaded apps that have an accessibility service or notification access (in India and some other countries); this one has neither, so take it if the full build won't install. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **Only one source's lines**: long-press a line and pick **Only xkcd** (its script, feed or app), or type `log only xkcd`, `log only feeds`. The log shows just those lines, new ones as they come, with a bar above the prompt, until ✕, back, `log all` or another command. Kinds: `commands`, `scripts`, `feeds`, `notifications`, `apps`, `calls`, `reminders`.
- **Search one source**: `/@xkcd` lists a script's lines, `/@hackernews rust` searches a feed's, `/@scripts error` every script's. Typing `/@` suggests them.

## Removed

- **The `── name · time ──` line before a script's or a feed's lines**: each line already shows its time (`log.time`) and ends with the script's or feed's name. A run's lines still start a group of their own. Those already in your log go, and a backup you restore doesn't bring them back.

## Fixed

- **Delete command and output** takes all of a command's output, a script's too: `xkcd random`'s comic comes when the script is done, sometimes under other lines, and now goes with the command. Copy output takes it too, and long-pressing a script's line offers the command that started it.

**kolahal-0.4.2-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
